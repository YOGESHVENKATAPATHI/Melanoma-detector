# Melano-CoAt: Melanoma Classification Using Hybrid Convolution-Attention Networks 
Project has done by Jothi Prakasam R, Yogesh V, Rajkumar N 
#
## This is our academic project and the paper has been published in the IEEE xplore portal. <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11539737">Vist our paper here</a>

Abstract—The diagnosis of melanoma is a problem that has not been easily resolved by existing methods because of the complex visual cues as well as high inter-class similarities between skin lesions. The accurate and early diagnosis of this type of skin cancer has a high impact on increasing the survival rate and improving quality of life. Traditional deep learning models have faced challenges in redundant feature learning as well as a lack of global understanding. It is for this reason that this work proposes a CoAtNet-based deep learning model for melanoma classification. CoAtNet is capable of seamlessly fusing convolutional operations for efficient local information learning with self-attention capabilities for modeling long-range dependencies. The proposed method is capable of achieving a high accuracy of 97.00% and a F1-score of 0.97. This work addresses challenges that arise in healthcare environments that require scalability as well as efficiency.
## The Problem
Melanoma is a deadly and dangerous form of skin cancer 
that originates from the pigment-producing cells of our skin, 
called melanocytes. It is caused by repetitive exposure to 
ultraviolet radiation, and such cancers tend to grow rapidly 
and migrate unnoticed to other parts of the body. It can appear 
as a suspicious mole or evolving skin lesion in which small 
changes in size, shape, or color may not raise suspicions. Early 
detection of these alterations can be a matter of life and death, 
since early diagnosis increases survival significantly. 
Regrettably, the diagnosis of melanoma is not that easy to 
make. A number of obstacles hinder early detection: 
differences in practitioners’ skill levels, lack of advanced 
diagnostic equipment, and an overloaded healthcare system. 
This could result in uncertainty, anxiety, and unnecessary 
treatment delays for patients. To close these gaps and provide 
everyone with an equal opportunity to combat this illness, 
affordable, reliable, and accurate solutions are desperately 
needed.

## Solution 
Melano-CoAt framework, which combines a deep learning network designed specifically for 
melanoma classification with dermoscopic images. The 
proposed work leverages convolutional feature learning and 
attention-based global context modeling integrated within a 
CoAtNet(Convolution Attention Networks) network structure 
that focuses on improving diagnostic accuracy without 
compromising computational efficiency.
